## Marketing

Marketing skills from coreyhaines31/marketingskills are installed in `.claude/skills/` (and `.agents/skills/` for other agents). Before any marketing task:

1. Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` — the product marketing context. Items marked **[CONFIRM]** are unverified; ask the user before relying on them in copy.
2. Check `docs/marketing/` for the current plan: `01-positioning.md` (advantages, OpenRouter comparison, pre-launch blockers), `02-icp.md` (customer profile, where to find them), `03-channels-and-copy.md` (channel plan and ready copy), `04-90-day-plan.md` (roadmap, KPIs, budget).
3. Log shipped learnings as dated entries under `## Learnings` in `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

Rules for Athandra copy: use model names and $/M-token prices, never "cheapest" or "unlimited"; never compare model count with OpenRouter; do not claim zero platform fee, Claude availability, or data policy until confirmed. Audience is US small software companies; write in English, discuss with the user in Chinese.
